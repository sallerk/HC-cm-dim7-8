HC-cm-dim7-8 -- CM types of abelian varieties of dimensions 7 and 8: the degenerate ones, and the Weil classes
                their Hodge classes would need
======================================================================================================================

For simple CM abelian varieties of dimension g = 7 and g = 8, and for all CM abelian varieties of dimension 7, the
programs enumerate the CM types up to conjugacy, find the degenerate ones (Hodge classes not generated by divisor
classes) and determine which Weil classes (for which CM fields, on which products of powers of A) would generate
their Hodge classes.  This extends the enumeration of the CM-sixfold work (repository HC-cm-sixfolds, folder
enumeration/) from g <= 6 to g = 7, 8; the model, the conventions and part of the code are shared with it
(sections 2 and 4).

Status: the results in section 1 carry status labels (defined there).  Not refereed by a human expert.

1. RESULTS
======================================================================================================================
Status labels: VERIFIED = an independent second program agrees (the checking programs of this repository are pure
Python with their own group and lattice code, no Sage/GAP); COMPUTED = one program only; CITED = taken from the
literature (section 9; section 8 says which sources were seen only in part); INFERENCE = an argument that was not
checked by a computation.

The computation has three stages:
  Stage A  g = 7, simple CM abelian 7-folds: G transitive, CM fields of degree 14       trans_enum.py 7
  Stage B  g = 8, simple CM abelian 8-folds: G transitive, CM fields of degree 16       run_par.py -> trans_enum.py 8
  Stage C  g = 7, all CM abelian 7-folds: all subgroups G of W(B7) containing rho      stageC.py, run_parC.py

R1  Simple CM abelian 7-folds are nondegenerate (Stage A).  [VERIFIED]
    63 transitive groups of degree 14; 19 of them have a central fixed-point-free involution (always unique), so
    there are 19 pairs (G, rho).  59 type orbits / 49 classes; the 52 primitive orbits (42 classes) all have d = 0;
    the 7 imprimitive orbits (types induced from the imaginary quadratic subfield) have d = 6.
    Checks: verify_trans.py (59/59 cases agree); the transitive part of Stage C has 19 classes with the same
    labels and group orders, 59 cases, 49 classes, 52 primitive with d = 0.  The result agrees with the known
    theorem that a simple CM abelian variety of prime dimension is nondegenerate (Ribet; see Gordon's survey,
    Thm 6.3(2), and the references there) [CITED].

R2  Simple CM abelian 8-folds: 257 primitive degenerate classes (Stage B).
    [COMPUTED by trans_enum.py; VERIFIED by verify_trans.py 8 for all 1943 groups and all 8869 cases]
    - 1954 transitive groups of degree 16; 1746 have a central fixed-point-free involution, 191 of them more than
      one; 1943 pairs (G, rho) (each involution up to conjugacy by the normalizer of G in S_16).
    - CM types: 8869 orbits / 5733 classes; primitive 7213 / 4784; primitive and degenerate 452 / 257.
    - Primitive degenerate by d (classes / orbits): d = 1: 46 / 78; d = 2: 195 / 330; d = 4: 16 / 44; never d = 3
      or d >= 5.  Imprimitive orbits: d = 4: 1225, d = 5: 13, d = 6: 310, d = 7: 108.
    - 173 pairs (G, rho) carry primitive degenerate types, with |G| from 16 to 80640; the largest are
      16T1873 = C2 x S8 and 16T1839 = C2 x A8 (both d = 1; E = K F, K imaginary quadratic, F of degree 8);
      16T1648 (order 4608) has d = 2.
    - Index of degeneracy n0 = 1 for all 257 classes: A itself carries Hodge classes that are not generated by
      divisor classes; the lowest cohomological degree of such a class (deg0) is 8 when d = 1 and 4 when d = 2 or
      d = 4.
    - Subfields: every d = 1 class has exactly one balanced imaginary quadratic subfield and no balanced quartic
      one; every d = 2 class has exactly one balanced quartic CM subfield K' (signature (2,2) at both embedding
      pairs); no d = 4 class has an imaginary quadratic or quartic CM subfield, and 10 of them have no proper CM
      subfield at all.
    - Abelian Galois groups (16T2, 16T4, 16T5): 5 classes, tiers (i) or (ii), n0 = 1, in agreement with Yanai's
      Thm 4.1 [CITED].

R3  Which Weil classes are needed (the tiers of the 257 classes).  [COMPUTED by weil_tiers.py (D <= 16) and
    ext_weil.py (D = 24, 32, 48); VERIFIED by verify_trans.py (D <= 16, all 452 degenerate orbits) and by
    verify_ext.py (16/16 classes at Dmax = 32, 4/4 at Dmax = 48)]
    D = dimension of the variety of Weil type (a sub-product of a power of A), see section 2.
    (i)   46 classes (d = 1): imaginary-quadratic Weil classes on A itself (D = 8).  Lambda_U = Z w_K for a unique
          imaginary quadratic K with signature (4,4).
    (ii)  195 classes (d = 2): Weil classes of a quartic CM subfield K' of E with signature (2,2) at both
          embedding pairs, on A itself (D = 8), in H^{2,2}(A).  In 37 of them two balanced imaginary quadratic
          subfields K1, K2 (K' = K1 K2) span Lambda_U over Q, but with lattice index 2; in the other 158 the
          imaginary-quadratic Weil characters have rank < 2 even at D = 16.
    (iii) 2 classes (16T52, 16T147; d = 4): Weil classes for an octic K' on A^2 (D = 16).
    (iv)  14 classes (d = 4): no lattice generation up to D = 16.  10 of them (16T6, 16T12, 16T28, 16T41, 16T45,
          16T135, 16T143, 16T157, 16T166, 16T395) are spanned over Q at D = 16 (octic K', index 2) and generated
          at D = 32 (fields of degree 8 and 16).  The other 4 (16T66, 16T425, 16T444, 16T748) have no Weil
          character at all up to D = 24, Q-span at D = 32 (index 8) and lattice generation at D = 48 (degrees 8,
          16, 24).  No imaginary quadratic or quartic Weil character occurs for a d = 4 class up to D = 48.
    So: (1) only the 46 classes of tier (i) are explained by imaginary-quadratic Weil classes on 8-folds;
    (2) 195 classes need quartic-CM Weil classes on A itself, and 16 classes (dim MT = 5, the minimum possible by
    the bound dim MT >= 2 + log2(dim A) of Ribet and Dodson [CITED]) need Weil classes on varieties of dimension 16
    to 48 for CM fields of degree 8, 16 or 24.  Within D <= 48 every class has a Weil explanation.  [VERIFIED]

R4  Consequence for the Hodge conjecture.  [INFERENCE; the results used are CITED]
    For the 257 classes the Hodge conjecture would follow from the algebraicity of the Weil classes of R3, which
    live on varieties of dimension D >= 8.  The known algebraicity results for Weil classes concern dimension 4
    (imaginary quadratic K) and split Weil type in dimension 6 (Markman, arXiv:2502.03415); none of them reaches
    the Weil classes needed here.  Exception (correction of 2026-10-05 to an earlier version of this statement):
    members that are factors of the Jacobian of a Fermat curve of degree 32, 40, 48 or 60 (CM by Q(zeta_m), see
    R6) are covered by the Hodge conjecture for Fermat varieties of these degrees (Aoki; Miranda, Movasati,
    Rufino, Villaflor, arXiv:2609.27301) together with Shioda's Theorem IV (Math. Ann. 245 (1979)).  For all
    other members the classification says which Weil classes would be needed, not that the Hodge conjecture
    holds.

2. MODEL AND CONVENTIONS
======================================================================================================================
As in the CM-sixfold enumeration (HC-cm-sixfolds, enumeration/README.txt):
- A CM algebra E of degree 2g with Galois closure L gives G = Gal(L/Q) acting on X = Hom(E, C) = {0, ..., 2g-1};
  complex conjugation acts as rho(i) = i+g mod 2g and is central in G, so G is a subgroup of W(B_g), the
  centralizer of rho in S_2g.  A CM type Phi contains one point of each pair {i, i+g}.  G is transitive iff E is a
  field.  Not every enumerated pair (G, Phi) needs to be realised by CM fields.
- Coordinates: X*(U_E) = Z^g via chi -> (chi_i - chi_{i+g})_{i<g}; Hodge cocharacters mu_h in {+1,-1}^g (h in G).
- d = g - rank{mu_h : h in G} = g + 1 - dim MT (the defect); Lambda_U [JSON field LU] = {chi in Z^g :
  chi . mu_h = 0 for all h}, saturated of rank d, stored in Hermite normal form.
- primitive: no non-trivial block system of G on the orbit O has Phi cap O as a union of blocks (the CM type is
  not induced from a proper CM subfield; the factor is simple).
- orbit = a CM type Phi up to G (one record per orbit; stored Phi = smallest representative).  class = Phi up to
  the normalizer N_W(G), i.e. the W(B_g)-conjugacy class of the pair (G, Phi) [JSON field Nclass].
- Stages A and B start from GAP's TransitiveGroups(2g): for each transitive group T with a central fixed-point-
  free involution rho' (each rho' up to conjugacy by the normalizer of T in S_2g), T is conjugated so that rho'
  becomes rho; the label TI = "2gTk" and the index j of rho' are stored.  Stage C takes one subgroup per conjugacy
  class of W(B7)/<rho> (GAP ConjugacyClassesSubgroups) and its preimage in W(B7); gid = index in that list.
- Imaginary quadratic subfields K: block systems with two blocks interchanged by rho; t = 2|Phi cap B_K| - |B_K|
  (B_K = the block containing point 0; the sign of t depends on this choice, |t| does not); K is balanced iff
  t = 0.  CM subfields K' of degree 2k: block systems with 2k blocks and rho(B) != B; for each rho-pair of blocks
  {B, rho B}, r = |Phi cap B| gives the signature (r, b - r) of K' at that embedding (b = block size); K' is
  balanced iff 2r = b for all pairs (then A carries a Weil structure for K').
- Weil classes (the model of general_weil2.py of the CM-sixfold enumeration, implemented here in weil_tiers.py and
  ext_weil.py): a CM field K' in the Galois closure corresponds to a subgroup M of G with rho not in M,
  [G:M] = [K':Q]; K' acts on sub-products of powers of the simple factors of A through the G-orbits on
  O x G/M ("units").  A combination of units with integer multiplicities (sign = conjugate action) is of Weil type
  iff it is balanced at every embedding of K'; its Weil characters lie in Lambda_U; its dimension D is the sum of
  |multiplicity| x (dimension of the unit).  The Weil classes for K' of degree 2k on a D-dimensional variety lie
  in cohomological degree D/k.  Dlat_X / Dq_X = smallest D at which the Weil characters of the fields of category
  X generate Lambda_U as a lattice / span it over Q, for X = IQ ([K':Q] = 2), le4 (<= 4), le8 (<= 8), all (<= 16).
  Searched up to D = 16, which is exhaustive within the model since balance forces [K':Q] <= D; ext_weil.py goes
  to D = 24, 32, 48 for the d >= 3 classes.  K' outside the Galois closure, auxiliary abelian varieties and
  constructions other than Weil classes are not considered.
- Tiers of the degree-16 classes (summarize_g8.classify): (i) Dlat_IQ = 8; (ii) otherwise Dlat_all = 8; (iii)
  otherwise Dlat_all = 16; (iv) otherwise.
- n0 (index of degeneracy) = smallest n such that A^n carries a Hodge class outside the algebra generated by
  divisor classes = min over nonzero v in Lambda_U of max_i |v_i|.  deg0 = smallest cohomological degree of such a
  class on A itself = min of sum_i |v_i| over nonzero v in Lambda_U with all |v_i| <= 1.
- Stage C uses the definitions of the CM-sixfold enumeration: reduced = all orbits primitive and no isogenous
  pair; strict KEY TEST = Lambda_U is generated by imaginary-quadratic Weil characters with each simple factor used
  at most once; for reduced pairs with d >= 1 the IQ multiplicity tier [mult: Dmin, D <= 14]; for reduced strict
  failures the general tier [general: Dmin (lattice), Dq (Q-span), searched at Dmax = 6, 8, ..., 16].

5. HOW TO RUN

everything from this folder.  Below, SAGE stands for

(on Windows with Git Bash: prefix MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 and use "$(pwd -W)" instead of "$PWD").  The pure-Python
scripts need only Python 3 and its standard library.  run_par.py and run_parC.py write into par_logs/.

8. NOT CHECKED INDEPENDENTLY; CAVEATS
======================================================================================================================
- Not independently verified: the N_W(G)-class labels (GAP normalizers); the TransitiveIdentification labels;
  that GAP's TransitiveGroups(16) and centre computations give every pair (G, rho) -- the correspondence between
  pairs (T, rho' up to the normalizer of T in S_16) and W(B8)-classes is INFERENCE, it was not matched against the
  subgroup lattice of W(B8), which is too large; the Stage C class list (one GAP route only; at g = 6 two routes
  agreed in the CM-sixfold enumeration).
- The Weil framework is the model of section 2: CM fields K' inside the Galois closure, acting on sub-products of
  powers of A, with lattice generation by Weil characters.  K' outside the closure, auxiliary abelian varieties
  and constructions other than Weil classes were not considered.  The search up to D = 16 is exhaustive within the
  model.
- Lattice versus Q-span matters (Milne, Thm 1.8 needs the exact lattice): the 37 tier-(ii) classes with
  imaginary-quadratic Q-span and the 10 tier-(iv) classes with Q-span at D = 16 have only "rational"
  explanations at the smaller D.
- Nothing at D >= 8 is covered by known algebraicity results, except the Fermat-type members of R4.  The
  classification says which Weil classes would be needed, not that the Hodge conjecture holds.  [INFERENCE]
- Realisability by actual CM fields was checked only for the examples listed in R6.
- n0 = 1 for every degree-16 class; the degree-16 case of the construction of Kida-Yanai 2022 was not compared.
- Signs of t and r depend on which block contains the point 0; the invariants are |t| and the unordered pairs
  {r, b - r}.
- The literature was read through text extracts of the sources; Kida-Yanai 2020 and 2022, Dodson and White were
  seen only as abstracts, first pages, reviews or as quoted by other sources.  Aoki's results are quoted as
  compiled by Miranda et al.  R4 uses the results of Markman, Aoki, Miranda et al. and Shioda as their authors
  state them.
- The list of reduced Stage C configurations with minimal Weil dimension > 6 is complete for the reduced pairs.
  Non-reduced pairs at g = 7 reduce to g <= 6, which the CM-sixfold enumeration covers.  [INFERENCE, the same
  argument as there]
- verify_generalC3.py rests on a reduction argument stated in its header [INFERENCE]; it covers the 6 cases at
  which verify_generalC.py was stopped.
